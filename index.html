Tucked inside Grandma’s wooden sewing box lies a secret smaller than a thimble, yet stronger than iron. CaSi, a small crocheted cactus holding magical keys in her yellow yarn pot, must lead her sewing box allies through midnight training to recover the hidden Star Keys before the Shadow King consumes the home’s memories.

Key lore

One key. A thousand secrets.

Every door in the house hides a world, and every world is guarded by something that wants to keep it shut. CaSi does not carry keys to open doors. She carries them to remember what lives behind them.

Every door hides a world.

The midnight pact

Every night at 12:00 the yarn wakes. Thirty nights is all CaSi has to train her allies and gather the keys.

See episode 01

The Star Keys

Ten keys, each one holding a memory of the house. Each is hidden behind a trial of needle, thread or shadow.

See episode 04

The Shadow King

He does not steal objects. He consumes memories, and the house forgets itself a little more each night.

See episode 07

The 30th night

A clock with thirty notches guards the front door lock. When the last notch strikes, the lock opens or stays shut forever.
