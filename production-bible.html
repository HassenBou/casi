The rules of the world of CaSi.

The world

Grandma’s wooden sewing box. At midnight its tools come alive and the house’s memories become something worth protecting.

The structure

Thirty nights, ten Star Keys, one Shadow King. Season 1 follows one key per episode, ending on the thirtieth night at the front door lock.

Tone

Handcrafted and warm at the centre, dark and mysterious at the edges. Yarn, thread and gold light against deep midnight blue.
